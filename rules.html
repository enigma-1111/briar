THE RULES

Read at the Gate. Nothing on this page is sold. No coin moves.

BRIAR IS THE TOLL

Briar is what you pay to pass a door. It is the name of the coin, and the coin is not minted. A toll is not a purse that grows. You do not stake it for a yield. You do not get a share because you stood in the street. When a swap is real, the city keeps a tenth at the Gate. Today that is only a note. No swap. No airdrop.

A KEY IS A BUILDING

A key is not a ticket. It is the house, the shop, or the workshop. The first named keys are Hearth, Loaf, The Cup, Quill, and Forge. A key opens the rooms and the box behind the counter. A tenant gets the sign, or the bed, or the day board. The tenant does not get the box. A lease stays if the building changes hands. The Cup rents one bed for a week, on this browser only. The renter does not take the inn.

TOWN PLACES ARE NOT FOR SALE

The Greenwood, the Chapel, the Gate, the Market, the Inn, the Well, and the School stay with the town. You may walk the wood. Oaks and one chapel. You cannot buy them. A market stall is not a parcel. Apple, Cloth, Salt, Wick may list one good for the day. The board clears at midnight. Hanging signs are painted wood, not keys. The Gate is the center. Parcels are cut slowly outward from it. No auction. Scarlet is the only street cut. Marian, John, and Much are drawn and locked.

AN EMPTY KEY CARRIES

An empty Scarlet key does not sit dead. Hearth, Loaf, The Cup, Quill, and Forge show a carrying fee of one wick a week while no block is on the floor. The note lives on this browser. Nothing is taken. No coin moves. A block on the floor means the key is occupied, and the carry rests. Noting the week is a mark, not a payment.

NO PROMISED PROFIT

No one is promised rent. No one is promised a return. A price on a door is a price the owner posts, not a promise the town makes. A job at the Gate is a job. It is not a yield. If the street is empty, it is empty. The town does not pay you for holding a key.
