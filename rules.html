RULES

  1. Briar is the coin name. It is not minted.
  2. The parcel is the unit. The Gate is the center. Cut slowly outward. No auction.
  3. First five: Hearth, Loaf, The Cup, Quill, Forge. Nothing else is named.
  4. The Greenwood, the Chapel, the Gate, the Market, the Inn, the Well, and the School stay unsold.
  5. Scarlet is the only street cut. Marian, John, and Much are drawn.
  6. A key opens rooms and a box. A renter gets the room, not the box.
  7. The Cup rents one bed for a week on this browser. The renter does not get the box.
  8. A swap note at the Gate says the city keeps a tenth. No real swap. No coin moves.
  9. No one is promised rent or profit.
