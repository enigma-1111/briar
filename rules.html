RULES

  1. Briar is the coin for every city. Norham is only the first.
  2. The Greenwood, the Chapel, the Gate, the Market, and the Inn are not for sale.
  3. Scarlet is the only street cut. Marian, John, and Much are drawn.
  4. A key opens rooms and a box. A renter gets the room, not the box.
  5. Swaps happen at the Gate. The city takes a small cut.
  6. No one is promised rent or profit.
