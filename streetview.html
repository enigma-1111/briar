Norham · Walk

SCARLET

A street view. The wood is on both verges. The doors face the cobbles.

The wood

Oaks stand off the lane. They are not keys. The Greenwood is the same wood, further in.

The doors

Hearth has Settle and Loft. Loaf has a floor and a back room. The Cup has four beds. Quill has a counter. Forge has a work floor. Last Door ends the cut street.

The walker

Look: a hood, a cloak, a hem. Colors are russet, moss, ink, wool, wine. One cloak. Not a shop.

Movement: the stick walks. Drag turns the head. The body follows. Walls stop the walker.

Functions: place one block on an open house. Rent a bed at the Cup. Rent Settle or Loft at Hearth. Paint a door name. Pass the key. Note the empty week.

Cloak: wool
